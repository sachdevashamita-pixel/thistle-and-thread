Our Story

Thoughtfully crafted textiles designed to bring warmth, comfort and timeless elegance into every home.

Born From a Love of Comfort

At Thistle & Thread, we believe that true luxury lies in the feeling of home. Every knitted throw, cushion and baby blanket is thoughtfully designed to bring softness, warmth and understated elegance into everyday living.

Inspired by timeless interiors and crafted with exceptional attention to detail, our collections celebrate comfort that lasts beyond seasons. We create pieces that become part of family moments, quiet evenings and cherished memories.

Wrap Life in Comfort

Our philosophy is simple. Comfort should never compromise on beauty. Every collection reflects timeless craftsmanship, premium materials and thoughtful design that enriches everyday living.
