Craftsmanship

Every Thistle & Thread piece is knitted, finished and quality checked in our own in-house facility.

Made In-House, Start to Finish

Every Thistle & Thread piece is proudly produced in our own manufacturing facility. This allows us to maintain the highest standards of quality while honouring the artistry behind every knitted creation.

From selecting premium yarns to the final inspection, every detail is carefully considered so each product reflects the warmth, elegance and craftsmanship our customers expect.

Our Process

From raw yarn to a finished piece ready for your home, every step is done by hand and by our own team.

Yarn Selection

We source premium natural and blended yarns, chosen for softness, durability and a beautiful drape.

Knitting

Each piece is knitted in-house by our skilled team, with close attention paid to tension, texture and pattern.

Finishing

Edges are finished, loose threads secured and every piece steamed for a soft, polished drape.

Quality Check

Before it ever reaches you, every throw, cushion and blanket is inspected by hand.
